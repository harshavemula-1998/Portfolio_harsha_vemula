03 Projects

Selected work

Featured · CIROH · Open source

NGEN-Datastream: NextGen National Water Model Research DataStream

I led DevOps and infrastructure automation for the Research DataStream, which has completed 68,000+ hydrologic model runs. Each day an EventBridge schedule starts a Step Functions workflow for every one of 21 VPUs. It launches a Graviton instance from a baked AMI, runs the DataStream CLI, polls until the run finishes, confirms the output in S3, and then tears everything down.
