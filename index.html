About Me

I am a versatile DevOps Engineer with over four years of experience building scalable infrastructure, automating cloud environments, and streamlining CI/CD pipelines. Currently working at the University of Alabama's Alabama Water Institute, I specialize in designing and implementing AWS-based research infrastructure with a focus on security, compliance, and cost optimization.

My goal is to continue contributing to innovative projects that leverage cloud-native technologies to solve complex challenges while maintaining operational excellence and security compliance.
